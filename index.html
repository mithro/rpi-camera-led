RPi Camera LED

Research and design for an LED indicator board that attaches to the Raspberry Pi camera flex cable (15 pin, 1.0 mm pitch FPC).

Research

Part availability research comparing JLCPCB's PCBA parts library and NextPCB's Rev0 service (which sources components from HQ Online's 600k+ in-stock inventory):

  • I2C controllable RGB LEDs
  • I2C GPIO expanders with LED driving capability
  • Cheap (< $0.50) MCUs with I2C and no external parts required
  • RPi camera compatible FPC connectors (1.0 mm pitch, 15 pin)
